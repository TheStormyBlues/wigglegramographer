Drag the green band edges or a red cut line. The frames sit on a fixed mechanical pitch, so even widths mean the cuts found the gaps — anything above 2% and they have probably missed.

100%

The region grows to match your point's distance from the camera, so it follows the subject instead of straddling foreground and background. Stabilise is how much of the measured shift to apply — under 100% keeps some drift for a looser wiggle, over 100% overshoots for a punchier one.

Fine tune

Correct any frame the alignment got wrong.

0, 0 px

Pick a frame with 1–4 or the filmstrip, then nudge with the arrow keys — Shift for bigger steps. Space pauses so you can flip between two frames and line them up by eye. Offsets carry into the export.

Export

Re-runs the real alignment at full resolution.

600

The preview averages optical flow; export runs full ECC. They usually agree, so the per-frame scores below are the receipt — anything under 0.80 did not lock and will visibly jump.
